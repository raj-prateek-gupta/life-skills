# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Q1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment can happen through **verbal, visual, or physical behaviour** of a sexual nature. Inappropriate comments about someone's clothing or body, sexual jokes, obscene language, and other unwanted sexual behaviour can cause sexual harassment.

The three main forms are:

1. **Verbal:** Sexual jokes, comments about someone's body or clothing, sexual remarks, innuendos, asking for sexual favors, repeatedly asking someone out, spreading sexual rumors, or using obscene language.
 
2. **Visual:** Sharing or displaying sexually explicit pictures, posters, drawings, cartoons, emails, messages, or other inappropriate content.
  
3. **Physical:** Unwanted touching, hugging, kissing, rubbing, blocking someone's movement, sexual gestures, staring, or sexual assault.

---

## Q2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

I would do the following things:

1. If I witnessed such behaviour, I would support the affected person and report what I observed rather than ignoring it.

2. If the behaviour is serious, repeated, or I feel unsafe, I would seek help from the appropriate authorities or workplace support system immediately.

3. I would **document the incident**, including the date, time, location, people involved, witnesses, and what happened. I would also preserve relevant emails, messages, or other evidence.
