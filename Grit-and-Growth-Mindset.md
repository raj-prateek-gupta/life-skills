# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1 — Grit

The video explains that success is not only about talent or intelligence. Grit means having passion for a long-term goal and continuing to work toward it with patience and perseverance, even when things become difficult.

## Question 2 — Introduction to Growth Mindset

A growth mindset means believing that our abilities and intelligence can improve through effort, learning, practice, and feedback. Instead of seeing failure as proof that we cannot do something, we should see it as an opportunity to learn and improve.

## Question 3 — What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, decisions, effort, and choices have a major influence on the results we achieve.

The key point of the video is to focus on what we can control instead of blaming external circumstances. Taking responsibility for our actions helps us stay motivated and take action toward our goals.

## Question 4 — What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).

- Believe in your ability to figure things out.
- Embrace challenges.
- Honor the struggle.
- Learn from failures and mistakes.
- Focus on improvement rather than perfection.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Keep trying when things become difficult.

## Question 5 — What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?

I can build a growth mindset by treating every difficult task as an opportunity to learn rather than assuming that I am not capable of doing it. As a software developer, I will focus on improving my skills consistently instead of expecting myself to understand everything immediately.

My actions will be:

- Practice coding every day, even if it is for a short time.
- Break difficult problems into smaller problems.
- Learn from my mistakes instead of getting frustrated by them.
- When I get stuck, try different approaches before giving up.
- Ask for feedback from mentors and teammates.

My main goal is to develop the habit of **learning, practicing, making mistakes, and trying again** instead of giving up when something feels difficult.
