# Tiny Habits

## Question 1

**In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?**

The most interesting idea was that habits do not need to start big. Starting with a tiny action makes a habit easier to repeat and grow over time.

## Question 2

**How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P?**

- **M — Motivation:** How much you want to do the habit.
- **A — Ability:** How easy the habit is to do.
- **P — Prompt:** A reminder or trigger to do the habit.

To make a habit easier, reduce the effort and connect it to an existing routine.

## Question 3

**Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of a habit?**

- Celebration creates a positive feeling.
- It helps the brain remember the behavior.
- It makes us want to repeat the habit.
- What we celebrate, we strengthen.

## Question 4

**In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?**

The most interesting idea was the **1% better every day** concept. Small improvements may seem insignificant at first, but repeated improvements can create a big change over time.

## Question 5

**What is the book's perspective about Identity?**

Atomic Habits says that lasting change starts with **identity**.

- Focus on who you want to become, not only what you want to achieve.
- Example: Instead of saying, "I want to read more," think, "I am a reader."

## Question 6

**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do.**

- Make it **obvious**.
- Make it **attractive**.
- Make it **easy**.
- Reduce the number of steps.
- Prepare everything in advance.

## Question 7

**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do.**

- Make the cue less visible.
- Add extra steps before doing it.
- Remove easy access to it.
- Create friction that makes the habit inconvenient.

## Question 8

**Pick one habit that you would like to do more of. What are the steps that you can take to make the cue obvious or the habit more attractive or easy and/or the response satisfying?**

**Habit: Study JavaScript every day.**

- **Obvious:** Keep my JavaScript notes on my desk.
- **Attractive:** Study topics that I enjoy.
- **Easy:** Start with just 20 minutes.
- **Satisfying:** Mark the habit as completed after studying.

## Question 9

**Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of. What are the steps that you can take to make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?**

**Habit: Excessive social media scrolling.**

- **Invisible:** Remove social media apps from my home screen.
- **Unattractive:** Remind myself that scrolling wastes valuable time.
- **Hard:** Log out and keep my phone away while studying.
- **Unsatisfying:** Track my unnecessary screen time.
