## What are active and diffuse modes of thinking?

### Active Mode

Active mode of thinking is a way of thinking about a particular problem in a focused manner with full concentration. This mode is active when we solve problems such as puzzles, mathematical problems, complex tasks, or when playing chess.

### Diffuse Mode

Diffuse mode of thinking is a more relaxed way of thinking, and it usually happens when we are not actively concentrating on a specific problem. In this mode, our brain is more relaxed and can make broader connections between different ideas. It can occur while traveling, walking, relaxing, or listening to music.
